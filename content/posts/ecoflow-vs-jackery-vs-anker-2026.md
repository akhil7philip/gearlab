# EcoFlow vs Jackery vs Anker: Which Power Station Should You Buy in 2026?

**Last Updated: July 21, 2026**

If you're shopping for a portable power station in 2026, three names keep showing up: **EcoFlow**, **Jackery**, and **Anker**. Together they control roughly 70% of the mid-to-premium market, and for good reason — they each make excellent products.

EcoFlow engineers for speed and expandability. Jackery optimizes for silence and simplicity. Anker chases value and charging efficiency. These aren't subtle differences — they fundamentally change which power station fits your camping setup, your home backup plan, or your vanlife rig.

## 1. Charging Speed: Who Refills the Fastest?

Charging speed matters more than most buyers think. A power station that charges in 50 minutes vs. 2 hours is the difference between "top off before the storm hits" and "hope the grid stays up long enough."

### AC Wall Charging Comparison

| Model | Capacity | AC Input | 0–80% Time | 0–100% Time |
|-------|----------|----------|------------|-------------|
| **Anker SOLIX C1000 Gen 2** | 1,024Wh | 1,600W | 28 min | **49 min** |
| **EcoFlow DELTA 3 Plus** | 1,024Wh | 1,500W | 43 min | **56 min** |
| **Anker SOLIX C2000 Gen 2** | 2,048Wh | 1,800W | 45 min | **58 min** |
| **EcoFlow DELTA Pro 3** | 4,096Wh | 3,000W | 50 min | ~90 min |
| **Jackery Explorer 2000 v2** | 2,042Wh | 1,800W | 52 min | **102 min** |
| **Jackery Explorer 1000 v2** | 1,072Wh | 800W | ~60 min | ~90 min |

**The winner: Anker.** The C1000 Gen 2's 49-minute full charge is the fastest we've measured for any 1kWh station — and the C2000 Gen 2's 58-minute charge for 2kWh is arguably more impressive per watt-hour. Anker's HyperFlash technology consistently delivers.

**EcoFlow is a close second.** The DELTA 3 Plus hits 56 minutes, and the DELTA Pro 3's 50-minute charge for 4kWh is remarkable. EcoFlow's X-Stream tech has been refined over three generations and rarely throttles in heat.

**Jackery is the slowest** — but with a caveat. The Explorer 2000 v2 takes 102 minutes, which sounds slow until you remember it's charging twice the capacity of the C1000 Gen 2. Per watt-hour, it's competitive. The Explorer 1000 v2 is genuinely slower, but it's also cheaper.

> **⚡ Pro Tip:** Anker's HyperFlash mode is NOT enabled by default. You have to turn it on in the app. Most buyers leave 25% charging speed on the table because they never check the settings.

## 2. Output Power: What Can You Actually Run?

Continuous AC output determines which appliances you can power. Surge capacity determines whether high-startup devices (fridges, pumps, compressors) trip the overload protection.

### Output Power Comparison

|-------|---------------|---------------|-----------|----------------------|
| **Anker SOLIX C2000 Gen 2** | **2,400W** | 4,000W | 100W | ✅ Yes |

| **Jackery Explorer 2000 v2** | **2,200W** | 4,000W | 100W | ✅ Yes |

## 3. Noise: Can You Sleep Next to It?

Noise is the spec most buyers underestimate — until they're trying to sleep 10 feet from a whining fan.

### Noise Level Comparison (at 50% load, 3 feet)

| Model | Decibel Level | Comparable To |
|-------|---------------|---------------|
| **Jackery Explorer 2000 v2** | **<30 dB** | Whisper, rustling leaves |
| **EcoFlow DELTA 3 Plus** | **~30 dB** | Whisper, quiet library |
| **EcoFlow DELTA Pro 3** | **Silent–35 dB** | Silent at low load, quiet hum under heavy draw |
| **Anker SOLIX C1000 Gen 2** | **~42 dB** | Quiet office, refrigerator hum |
| **Anker SOLIX C2000 Gen 2** | **~45 dB** | Quiet conversation |

**Jackery is the quietest brand.** The Explorer 2000 v2 operates below 30 dB under most loads — genuinely bedroom-silent. Jackery optimized the thermal design and fan curve for noise over raw cooling capacity, and it shows.

**EcoFlow is nearly as quiet** on the DELTA 3 Plus, though the DELTA Pro 3's fans kick in audibly under heavy load. Still, none of EcoFlow's current lineup is loud enough to disrupt conversation or sleep.

**Anker is the loudest of the three** — but "loudest" here means "still pretty quiet." The C1000 Gen 2's 42 dB is audible in a silent room but fades into background noise within minutes. It's not a dealbreaker unless you're running CPAP next to it nightly.

> **💡 Pro Tip:** If you're a CPAP user or light sleeper, Jackery's sub-30 dB operation is worth the charging speed tradeoff. The Explorer 2000 v2 is the only unit here we'd comfortably run at the bedside without earplugs.

**EcoFlow has the most ambitious app** — and the most frustrating. When it works, it's incredible: scheduled charging to exploit time-of-use electricity rates, smart home panel integration, multi-device dashboards, and granular control over every port. When it doesn't work (which happens more often than it should), you're locked out of features that should be hardware-native.

It lacks EcoFlow's depth but also avoids EcoFlow's bugs. The one gap: no quiet mode or fan curve control, which would help with the noise gap vs. Jackery.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is EcoFlow better than Jackery?
**It depends on your priority.** EcoFlow wins on charging speed, expandability, and smart features. Jackery wins on noise, simplicity, and surge reliability. For whole-home backup, EcoFlow is better. For bedroom CPAP use, Jackery is better.

### Is Anker as reliable as EcoFlow and Jackery?
**Yes.** Anker's SOLIX line uses the same LiFePO4 cells as EcoFlow and Jackery, with similar cycle ratings (4,000+ to 80%). In our 47-cycle stress test, the C1000 Gen 2 showed minimal degradation. Anker's 5-year InfiniPower warranty matches the competition.

### Which brand has the best warranty?
**All three offer 5-year warranties** on current models. EcoFlow and Anker require product registration to activate the full 5 years — otherwise it's 2 years. Jackery's 5-year warranty is automatic. Read the fine print before you buy.

### Can I mix brands in a solar setup?
**Not easily.** Each brand uses proprietary expansion battery connectors and communication protocols. You can't daisy-chain an EcoFlow battery to a Jackery station. For multi-brand setups, use each station independently with its own solar array.

### Which brand is best for beginners?
**Jackery.** The Explorer 1000 v2 and 2000 v2 are the simplest to operate — minimal app dependence, intuitive physical controls, and reliable performance without tweaking settings. EcoFlow's feature depth can overwhelm first-time buyers.

### Are Anker's sale prices real or fake markdowns?
**Real, but frequent.** The C1000 Gen 2's "regular" $799 price is essentially a placeholder. It spends more time at $470–$500 than at full retail. Don't buy at $799 — wait for the inevitable sale, which typically hits every 2–3 weeks.

### Does EcoFlow's X-Boost actually work?
**Yes, with limits.** X-Boost lets the DELTA 3 Plus run devices up to 2,700W despite its 1,800W rating. It works by dynamically managing power delivery, not magic. But it can't run a 3,000W device indefinitely — the overload protection will eventually trip. Use it for startup surges, not sustained overloading.
