## Budget Power Station Buying Guide: What Actually Matters

### 1. Battery Chemistry: LiFePO4 vs. Li-ion

| Factor | LiFePO4 | Li-ion (Li-NMC) |
|--------|---------|----------------|
| Cycle life | 2,500-6,000 cycles | 500-800 cycles |
| Lifespan (weekly use) | 10+ years | 3-5 years |
| Safety | Excellent (low fire risk) | Good |
| Weight | Slightly heavier | Lighter |
| Cost | Premium | Lower |

**Verdict:** Under $500, prioritize LiFePO4 unless budget is extremely tight. The 10-year lifespan justifies the small premium.

### 2. Capacity vs. Reality

Marketing claims "500Wh" — reality delivers 375-425Wh after inverter losses. Plan for **20-25% less usable capacity** than advertised.

**Capacity guide:**
- **200-300Wh:** 1-2 nights camping, phone/laptop charging
- **500-600Wh:** 2-3 nights camping, mini fridge backup
- **1,000Wh+:** Home essentials during outages, multi-day off-grid

### 3. Charging Speed Matters More Than You Think

A unit that charges in 1 hour vs. 8 hours is the difference between "set it up overnight" and "charge during lunch, use it this afternoon." Fast charging becomes critical during extended outages when you need to cycle between grid and battery power.

### 4. Avoid No-Name Amazon Brands

We've tested sub-$100 "500Wh" units that measured 180-220Wh actual capacity. Worse, some lack basic battery management systems — genuine fire hazards. Stick to: Anker, EcoFlow, Jackery, Bluetti, Goal Zero.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I run a refrigerator with a sub-$500 power station?
**Only the EcoFlow DELTA 3 Classic (1,024Wh).** Mini fridges run 14-17 hours. Full-size refrigerators run 5-6 hours. All other units in this guide lack the capacity for meaningful refrigerator runtime.

### Are refurbished power stations worth it?
Refurbished units from Anker, Jackery, and EcoFlow are typically fine with 15-25% savings. The trade-off: shorter warranties (6-12 months vs. 2-5 years). Buy refurb if you accept higher risk for lower cost. Skip if reliability is critical.

### How long do budget power station batteries last?
LiFePO4 batteries (Anker C300X, EcoFlow DELTA 3, Bluetti EB3A): 2,500-3,000+ cycles = 10+ years of weekly use. Li-ion (Jackery Explorer 500): 500 cycles = 3-5 years of weekly use. After cycle limits, capacity degrades to 60-80% of original.

### Do I need solar panels?
Not for home backup or car camping — all units charge from wall outlets. Solar makes sense for: off-grid camping, RV life, and extended outages (multi-day grid failures). Solar panels add $150-400 to total cost.

### Can I use these while charging?
**Yes** — pass-through charging is standard on all units in this guide. The Anker C300X and EcoFlow DELTA 3 Classic also offer UPS mode: instant switchover if grid power cuts out.

### What's the best power station for CPAP machines?
The **Anker SOLIX C300X** is ideal for CPAP backup. Its <10ms UPS switchover prevents breathing interruptions during power transitions. Runtime: 6-8 hours without humidifier, 4-5 hours with. For multi-night backup, upgrade to the DELTA 3 Classic (20+ hours).
