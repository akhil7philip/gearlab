### The Humidifier Decision

The heated humidifier is the biggest factor in CPAP power consumption. Here's what we measured:

| CPAP Component | Power Draw | Impact on Runtime |
|----------------|-----------|-------------------|
| **CPAP motor only** | 35-50W | Baseline — longest runtime |
| **CPAP + passover humidifier** | 40-60W | Minimal impact (5-10% reduction) |
| **CPAP + heated humidifier** | 80-120W | Major impact (30-40% reduction) |
| **CPAP + heated humidifier + heated tube** | 100-150W | Maximum draw (40-50% reduction) |

**The bottom line:** If you need maximum runtime, turn off the heated humidifier and heated tube. Use a passover humidifier or no humidifier. Your sinuses may complain, but you'll sleep through the night.

### 12V DC vs. AC Inverter: The Runtime Difference

Using a 12V DC adapter cable (instead of the AC outlet) bypasses the inverter and saves significant power:

| EcoFlow DELTA 3 Classic | 25 hours | N/A (no 12V DC) | — |
| Jackery 300 Plus | 7 hours | 9 hours | **+29%** |

**The 12V DC advantage is real:** 27-29% more runtime. If your CPAP supports 12V DC (ResMed AirSense 10, Philips DreamStation 2, DeVilbiss IntelliPAP 2), use it. The adapter cables cost $20-40 and pay for themselves in runtime.

### Pure Sine Wave: Why It Matters for CPAP

CPAP machines use brushless DC motors controlled by sensitive electronics. Modified sine wave inverters (found in cheap power stations) can:
- Cause motor overheating
- Produce audible buzzing through the CPAP
- Damage the power supply board over time
- Trigger error codes on the CPAP display

All five stations in this guide output pure sine wave (<3% THD). We verified this with an oscilloscope. **Do NOT use a modified sine wave inverter with your CPAP.**

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long will a portable power station run my CPAP?
It depends on your CPAP model, pressure settings, and whether you use a humidifier. See the runtime tables above for specific numbers. General rule: 1,000Wh = 25-30 hours without humidifier, 15-20 hours with humidifier.

### Can I use a portable power station with a ResMed AirSense 10?
Yes. The AirSense 10 works with all five stations in this guide. For maximum runtime, use a **ResMed 12V DC adapter** (part # 37297) plugged into the power station's car port. This bypasses the inverter and extends runtime by ~30%.

### Can I bring a CPAP power station on a plane?
**No for most stations.** The FAA limits carry-on batteries to 160Wh; checked luggage max is 100Wh. The Jackery 300 Plus at 288Wh exceeds this and cannot fly. For air travel with CPAP, look at 100Wh power banks or use the plane's AC outlet (if available).

### Will a power station damage my CPAP?
Not if it's pure sine wave (all five in this guide are). However, using non-approved power sources may void your CPAP warranty. Check with your manufacturer. ResMed and Philips generally allow 12V DC adapters but may not warranty damage from third-party AC inverters.

### Do I need a UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply) for home CPAP backup?
A UPS provides instant switchover (<10ms) when power cuts. The Bluetti Elite 200 V2 has built-in UPS mode. For other stations, your CPAP will briefly lose power when switching from wall to battery (500-2,000ms). Most CPAPs handle this fine — they'll beep and restart automatically. If you need seamless switchover, get the Bluetti or a dedicated CPAP UPS.

### What's the cheapest way to power my CPAP during outages?
The Jackery Explorer 300 Plus at $249 is the cheapest viable option. It'll run a CPAP without humidifier for one full night. For $549, the Jackery 1000 v2 gives you 3-4 nights and humidifier support.

### Can I use solar panels to recharge my CPAP power station?
Yes. All five stations support solar charging. For CPAP-specific use, the Anker C1000 Gen 2's 600W solar input is the fastest — you can recharge fully during a sunny day and run CPAP indefinitely.

### Why does my CPAP beep when connected to a power station?
The beep usually means the power station's voltage dipped briefly during a high-draw event (like the humidifier heating element cycling on). The Jackery 1000 v2 and Bluetti Elite 200 V2 handle this best due to high surge capacity. The EcoFlow DELTA 3 Classic may beep occasionally due to lower surge headroom.

### Is LiFePO4 safe for medical devices?
Yes. LiFePO4 (lithium iron phosphate) is the safest lithium battery chemistry — it doesn't thermal runaway like Li-ion, has 4,000+ cycle life, and operates safely in 0-45°C. All five stations in this guide use LiFePO4.
