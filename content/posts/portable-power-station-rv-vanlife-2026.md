## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I run my RV air conditioner from a portable power station?
**Only the EcoFlow DELTA Pro 3** can run a 13,500 BTU rooftop AC for 3+ hours. For shorter bursts (cooling down before bed), the Jackery 2000 v2 or Bluetti Elite 200 v2 can handle 1-2 hours. Smaller units won't run RV AC at all. For AC off-grid, you need either the DELTA Pro 3 or a soft start kit + smaller unit.

### What's the best power station for a Sprinter van build?
The **Anker SOLIX C1000 Gen 2** hits the sweet spot for Sprinter builds: 23.9 lbs (manageable), 1,024Wh (1-2 days), 2,000W output (appliance-capable), and 600W solar input (matches typical roof space). For full-timers, upgrade to the Jackery 2000 v2 or add expansion batteries.

### How do I charge a power station while driving?
All units charge from 12V car outlets (cigarette lighter), but slowly (100-150W). For faster charging, install a **DC-DC charger** wired directly to your van's starter battery or alternator. These deliver 40-60A (500-700W) and recharge a 1,000Wh unit in 2-3 hours of driving.

### Can I use these in freezing temperatures?
LiFePO4 batteries lose ~20-30% capacity below freezing. The units in this guide will discharge in cold weather, but charging is typically blocked below 32°F to protect the battery. Store your power station inside the heated van, not in an exterior compartment, for winter use.

### Do I need an inverter if I have a power station?
**No — the power station IS the inverter.** All units here output 120V AC through built-in pure sine wave inverters. You can plug RV appliances directly into the AC outlets. For 12V devices (fridge, lights, fan), use the 12V car outlet or USB ports.

### What's the difference between a power station and a "solar generator"?
**Marketing terminology only.** "Solar generator" usually means a power station bundled with solar panels. The unit itself is identical. We use "power station" throughout because it's more accurate — these don't generate power, they store it.

### Can I connect a power station to my RV's existing battery system?
**Not directly**, but you have options:
- **Shore power inlet:** Use the power station's AC output → RV shore power cord. Your RV's converter will charge the house battery from the power station.
- **DC-DC charger:** Some advanced setups use the power station's 12V output to charge the RV house battery via a DC-DC charger.
- **Replace the battery:** The ultimate upgrade — remove your lead-acid RV battery and use a power station as your entire electrical system.

### How long do these last in real vanlife use?
LiFePO4 units (all in this guide) are rated for 3,000-4,000+ cycles. In vanlife terms:
- **Full-timer (daily cycling):** 8-11 years
- **Part-timer (3-4x/week):** 15-20 years
- **Weekend warrior (2x/month):** 30+ years (you'll replace it for technology, not battery failure)
